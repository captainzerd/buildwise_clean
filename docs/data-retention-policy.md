# WyseBrix Data Retention Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-03-21

This document describes what user data WyseBrix collects, how long it is retained, and your rights as a user.

## Data We Collect

- **Account information:** Name, email address, phone number, professional profile
- **Project data:** Project details, cost entries, phase progress, photos
- **Contracts and invoices:** Signed contracts, payment records
- **Login activity:** Device type, sign-in method, timestamp (not location)
- **Audit logs:** Record of significant account and project actions

## Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Account & profile | Until account deletion | Required to provide the service |
| Project data | Until project deletion + 30 days | Recovery window |
| Contracts & invoices | 7 years | Legal / financial compliance |
| Login activity | 90 days | Security monitoring |
| Audit logs | 2 years | Accountability and dispute resolution |
| Deleted account data | 30 days | Final deletion processing window |

## Your Rights

**Export your data:** You can request a full export of your personal data from the Account settings page. Exports are limited to 3 per day.

**Delete your account:** You can submit an account deletion request from Account → Settings → Delete Account. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of the request being approved.

**Data correction:** You can update your profile information at any time from the Account settings page.

## Automatic Deletion

An automated process runs daily and permanently deletes:
- Login activity records older than 90 days
- Audit log entries older than 2 years
- Soft-deleted project data older than 30 days

## Contact

For data-related enquiries, contact support through the WyseBrix app.
